RestJot privacy policy

Local sleep data

Sleep records, tags, plans, reminders, preferences and your privacy choices are stored and processed on your device. The app does not upload sleep records to our servers or send them to Google Play. There is no app account, advertising SDK or remote analytics service.

Google Play purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing to query product prices and purchase status and to handle purchases and restoration of lifetime access. Google processes account, transaction and relevant device information under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The app receives product and purchase information from Google Play and keeps a local access flag. We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase access is separate from sleep-data backups and can be restored using the relevant Google Play account.

Your choices and local files

You can export or import sleep-data backups using the system file picker. Exported files may contain personal sleep information; keep them safe. Files are shared only with destinations you choose. Reminder notifications require device permission. If you copy the support email, it is written to the system clipboard. You can delete records within the app or clear app data or uninstall the app; delete exported files separately. Google may retain transaction information under its own policies.

Policy updates and contact

Updates will be published in the app. For questions, contact TofuKnifeDev@outlook.com.

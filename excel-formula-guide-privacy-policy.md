Last updated: November 20, 2026

## Information used by the app

Excel Formula Guide is an offline reference for spreadsheet formulas, templates, and checklists. It has no account, advertising, or analytics. We do not receive app usage data on a developer-operated server.

The app saves favorites, recently viewed items, and language, theme, and layout preferences in private app storage so it can restore them. Android may back up or transfer these settings and saved items when system backup or device transfer is enabled. Android manages and protects system backups under the device and account settings. We do not operate or access these backups.

When you choose to copy a formula or steps, the app writes the requested text to the clipboard. Your device manages the clipboard. The app does not read its contents.

When you share or save a spreadsheet template, the app creates a temporary copy in its private cache and opens the Android share sheet. If you choose another app or service, that recipient handles the file under its own terms. The app does not upload the file to us.

## Retention and deletion

You can remove favorites in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app’s local data. Android may restore a previously backed-up copy during reinstall or device setup. Manage or delete system backups under your device or Google account settings. Android may clear cached template copies, and you can clear them in the device’s app settings. There is no app account to delete.

## External services and contact

Rating the app opens the store. Contacting us opens a mail app. If you send an email, we receive the address and contents you send and use them to respond to and manage that request. The email provider and any destination app you choose have their own privacy practices.

We will update this policy when our practices change and publish the revised date here. For questions or privacy requests, contact Shenzhen Vector 123 Technology Company Limited at tofuknifedev@outlook.com.

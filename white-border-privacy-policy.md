# White Border — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

White Border (“the App”) is operated by Shenzhen Vector 123 Technology Company Limited (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the App.

## 1. Photos and Editing Content

The App accesses photos you select or open for editing, along with image metadata needed for features such as photo information decorations. Photo editing is performed on your device. We do not upload your photos or edited images to our servers.

The App may store temporary image files, downloaded fonts, editing preferences, custom text, and purchase or activation status on your device to provide its features.

When you choose to share an image, it is provided to the app or service you select. That service handles the image under its own privacy policy.

## 2. Permissions and Clipboard

Depending on your Android version and the feature you use, the App may request access to photos or storage to open and save images. Notification permission may be requested for supported download or task notifications. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

The App reads clipboard text when you choose to paste it and writes text when you use a copy action. It does not monitor your clipboard in the background.

## 3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded and interstitial ads.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud. IP addresses may be used to estimate your general location.

Where applicable, the App presents Google’s consent form. You can revisit your choices through the App’s privacy options when that entry is available. You can also manage advertising identifiers through your Android settings.

For details, see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Mobile Ads data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## 4. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App integrates Microsoft App Center Analytics and Crashes to understand feature usage and diagnose problems.

Information sent to App Center may include an installation identifier, device model, operating system and app versions, language, session information, feature interaction events, and crash or error details. Interaction events include actions such as selecting images, applying editing features, saving, sharing, and interacting with purchases or ads.

We do not intentionally include your photos, edited images, or the text you add to images in analytics events.

For details, see [App Center’s data collection documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/appcenter/sdk/data-collected) and [Microsoft’s Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement).

## 5. Purchases and Online Features

In Google Play versions, purchases and restoration are handled through Google Play Billing. The App processes purchase information to determine whether paid features are unlocked. We do not receive your payment card details.

In versions that support activation codes, the code you enter and the resulting activation token are sent to our server for verification. The token is stored locally to maintain activation status.

The App also makes network requests for features such as font downloads, quotes, update information, and promotional content. These requests disclose your IP address and standard connection information to the servers providing the requested content.

## 6. Sharing and Retention

We do not sell your photos or editing content. Information is disclosed to the service providers described above as needed for their functions, or when you choose to share content or contact us.

Local App data can generally be removed by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it, subject to Android’s data retention options. Images exported to shared storage remain until you delete them separately. Clearing local data does not delete records already held by Google, Microsoft, or other external services.

External services handle information according to their own policies. If you contact us, we process your email address and the information you provide to respond and resolve your request.

## 7. Your Choices

You can manage permissions and advertising settings through Android, use the App’s available advertising privacy options, remove local App data, and delete exported images.

You may contact us with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you. Our ability to fulfil a request depends on the information involved and applicable requirements.

## 8. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or its services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

## Information handled on your device

When you create an entry, the app handles the date and time, rating, and any optional information you enter, such as duration, orgasm status, tags, positions, and notes. This information can reveal sensitive details about your intimate life or health. The app uses it to display and search your journal and calculate summaries, charts, and patterns on your device. The app also stores your preferences and backup status locally. If you enable the app lock, it stores a salted PIN verifier. Your PIN is never stored in plain text.

Journal entries and settings are stored in Android's private app storage. The app has no account, developer-operated sync, advertising, or analytics. The Android app excludes its private data from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. We do not receive your journal entries. The app lock limits access to the app on the device. Keep your device protected with its own screen lock.

## Backups, exports, and external services

You choose whether to create a backup and where to save it. Backups use AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with a key derived from your backup password using Argon2id. The app does not store that password. To restore a backup, you select a file and enter its password. The app decrypts and checks it on your device, then replaces your current records after you confirm.

CSV exports contain readable, unencrypted data. Export and import use Android's system file picker and the document provider you choose. If you save an export to a cloud location or share it yourself, the recipient or service handles it under its own terms. Files you export can remain at the location you chose after you uninstall the app. Delete those copies separately if you no longer need them.

The app uses Flutter and supporting components for local storage, preferences, Android file selection, app version information, and opening external links. Opening the privacy policy, store listing, or your email app is your choice. The support action opens an email draft without attaching your records. If you send a support message, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and retain it only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid including intimate records in support email.

## Your choices and data retention

You can view, edit, or delete individual entries, export your data, or clear the journal from Settings. Your journal remains on your device until you delete it, replace it with a restored backup, clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall. We cannot look up or recover your on-device records. Contact us to ask about the information you sent to support or to request its correction or deletion.

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Its summaries are for personal reflection. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We will update the date above when this policy changes.

# Baby Look & Learn Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026. Last updated November 21, 2026.

## Scope

This policy covers Baby Look & Learn for Android (com.vector123.lexue.babypicturelearning), operated by Shenzhen Vector 123 Technology Company Limited. The app is designed for children ages 3 to 8.

## Learning and device settings

Pictures, quizzes and animal sounds are included with the app. Learning activities run on the device. Language and appearance preferences are stored by the app on the device. Android may back up or transfer these preferences according to your device and backup settings. We do not receive or access those backups.

The app does not require an account and does not include ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. We do not receive learning activity or device identifiers from the app. Google Play processes the purchase of the app under Google’s policies. The app does not request or handle payment details.

## Speech and external apps

For read-aloud, the app passes its built-in learning text to your Android device's text-to-speech engine and prefers an installed voice marked for offline use. Depending on your engine and voice settings, the engine may use the internet to process that text under its provider's privacy policy. The app does not record audio or send typed or spoken input to the engine. When the app cannot find a matching offline voice, it displays a message and other learning activities remain available.

If you choose to rate the app or open an attribution link, your device opens a separate app or website. These services handle activity under their own privacy policies.

## Contacting us and children's privacy

Parents and guardians can choose to contact us or report a problem. The app opens an email draft in your email app. It does not send the draft or attach learning activity. If you send the email, we receive the sender address and the information you choose to include, use it to respond, and retain it only as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. Please avoid including children's personal information. If you believe we have received personal information from a child, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

## Your choices and contact

You can remove local app settings by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Android backup copies are managed in your device or backup account settings. To ask about this policy or request access to or deletion of personal information supplied to us by email, contact tofuknifedev@outlook.com.

We will post changes to this policy at the same public address and update the effective date.

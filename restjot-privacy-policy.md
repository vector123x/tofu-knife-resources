RestJot privacy policy

Local sleep data

Sleep records, tags, plans, reminders, preferences and your privacy choice are stored and processed on your device. The app does not upload your sleep records to our servers or send them to Google Play. There is no app account, advertising SDK or remote analytics service.

Google Play purchases

After you accept this policy, the app uses Google Play Billing to query product prices and purchase status, make purchases and restore lifetime access. Google processes purchases through its services under its own terms and privacy policy, including account, transaction and relevant device information. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The app receives product and purchase information from Google Play and keeps a local access flag. We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase access is separate from sleep-data backups and can be restored using the relevant Google Play account. Sleep records are not required to buy or restore access.

Your choices and local files

You can export or import sleep-data backups using the system file picker. Exported files may contain personal sleep information; keep them safe. Files are shared only with destinations you choose. Reminder notifications require your device permission. You can change preferences and withdraw consent from the privacy page; the app then stops new payment requests and exits. Google may retain transaction information under its own policies.

Policy updates and contact

Material changes will be described in the app. For questions, contact TofuKnifeDev@outlook.com.

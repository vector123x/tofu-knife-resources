Basic Statement

Shenzhen Vector 123 Technology Company Limited ("we", "us", or "our") operates Pose Camera ("the Application"). We respect and protect your privacy. The Application is an offline pose reference and camera overlay tool. Pose templates and photo tips are bundled with the installation package and can be browsed without an account or network connection. We do not operate a user account system or ask you to provide identity information. We do not upload your photos, camera previews, favorites, shooting plans, settings, or usage content to our servers.

Permissions and Local Processing

The Application may request access to the camera so that you can take photos with pose overlays. Camera previews and captured photos are processed on your device. The Application does not request microphone permission, does not record audio, and does not collect voice or sound data.

When you use a custom reference, the system photo picker lets you voluntarily select one image. It is used only as a local camera overlay, orientation-corrected, and may be downsampled before a temporary copy is stored in the Application directory. The image is not uploaded or added to the gallery, and the Application does not request access to your entire photo library. The copy is deleted after you normally leave the camera; remnants from an interrupted session are cleared at the next launch.

When you choose to save a captured photo, the Application may access the system photo library or media storage only for the purpose of saving that file. Favorites, recent captures, shooting plans, theme preferences, privacy consent status, and optional Pro unlock status (where available) are stored locally on your device.

In-App Purchases

On supported platforms, you may optionally purchase Pose Camera Pro through the applicable app store on your device to unlock unlimited pose-assisted shooting, unlimited custom-reference shooting, and unlimited shooting plans. Payment processing is handled entirely by that platform store. We do not receive or store your payment card numbers or other full payment credentials. The store may provide purchase confirmation and entitlement status needed to unlock Pro features on your device.

Data Sharing and Transfers

Our core photo features process your content locally. Advertising on Android involves the third-party processing described below. Because the core Application functions run locally, we do not transmit your photos or app content to cloud services operated by us. If you choose to contact us by email, open an app store listing, or leave a review, any information you provide through those channels is handled according to the relevant platform or service.

Advertising on Android

The free Google Play version on Android displays Google AdMob banner ads on the pose home screen. Pro users do not see ads, and the app does not request further ads after a Pro purchase or restoration is confirmed. Other platforms do not use this advertising integration.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location); device and advertising identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID; app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views; and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, perform advertising analytics, prevent fraud and maintain its services. Advertising data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Camera previews, photos and custom reference images are not provided to the advertising SDK by the Application. We do not add Firebase Analytics or our own remote usage tracking through this integration.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks applicable consent requirements. Where required, a consent message allows you to make advertising privacy choices. Ads are requested only when UMP indicates they may be requested; your choices can affect ad personalization. When required, you can revisit these choices using “Ad privacy options” in Settings. UMP stores consent information on your device; the Application only caches ad eligibility in memory during the current run. Core pose browsing and photo features remain available if advertising or consent services cannot connect.

Google processes advertising data under its own privacy practices. For details, see https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's retention practices; clearing local app data does not necessarily delete data already processed by Google. You can revisit the available UMP privacy options in Settings and reset or delete your Android advertising ID using the advertising controls in your device settings. Withdrawing an advertising choice affects subsequent processing where applicable and does not itself delete data Google has already processed. For requests concerning data handled by Google, use the controls and request channels described in Google’s privacy policy; you can contact us using the email below for questions about the Application’s handling of data.

Data Retention and Control

Locally stored app data remains on your device unless you delete it in the Application, clear app data through the operating system settings, or uninstall the Application. Photos saved to your system gallery are managed by your device's photo library and storage settings.

# Smart Reply Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy applies to the Google Play version of **Smart Reply Assistant**, operated by Shenzhen Vector 123 Technology Company Limited. You can contact us at **tofuknifedev@outlook.com**.

## Chat text, clipboard, and preferences

Reply matching runs on your device. The app uses the message you type or paste to find matching replies. It keeps up to eight recent inputs in private app storage so you can reuse them. The app also stores the IDs of scripts and topics you save, the reply style you choose, and, where Lifetime Unlock is available, a count of successful free uses.

The app reads plain text from the clipboard when you tap **Paste**. When you tap **Copy**, it writes the reply or other content you selected to the clipboard. If your mail client or app store cannot be opened, the app may copy the corresponding email address or store link so you can open it yourself.

We keep this information on your device for these features and do not send it to our own servers, sell it, or use it for advertising. The app has no developer-operated user accounts and contains no advertising or general-purpose app analytics SDK.

## Google Play purchases

Where Lifetime Unlock is available, Google Play Billing handles product availability, localized prices, checkout, payment details, and refunds under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app can check your purchase status with Google Play at startup, when you return to the app, and when you restore or make a purchase. Google Play Billing may process billing diagnostics as part of operating that service.

The app uses product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens provided by Google Play to check, acknowledge, and restore your entitlement. It sends the purchase token to Google Play when acknowledging a purchase and keeps the unlock status in private app storage. We do not receive payment card details or send your chat text to Google Play through the billing service. We do not operate a server that receives your purchase tokens.

## External apps and email

Settings can open the system browser, a mail client, or an app store. The app does not attach your chat history to those requests. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your inquiry and keep them for as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

## Retention, security, and deletion

The app stores reply history and preferences in the app's private storage. Recent inputs stay there until they are replaced by newer items or you delete them. You can clear reply history in **Settings**, remove individual favorites, or clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove local records. We do not provide our own cloud sync service.

Clearing local app data does not delete Google Play transaction records. You can use Restore Purchase, where available, to recover the entitlement with the Google Play account used for the purchase. Contact Google Play about records held by Google.

## Children

The app is designed for everyday conversation and is not directed at children. If you believe a child sent us personal information, you can contact us to request deletion.

## Policy updates and contact

We post updates at the same address linked from the app's settings and update the effective date. To ask a privacy question or request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us, email **tofuknifedev@outlook.com**.

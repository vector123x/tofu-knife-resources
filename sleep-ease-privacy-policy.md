This policy covers the Google Play version of Random Word Sleep (com.vector123.sleepwords), a relaxation and sleep-support app for adults.

Information processed on your device

The app stores your language choice, playback and breathing preferences, selected word categories, blocked words, recently played word identifiers and last-session settings in its private local storage. These records support your settings and listening experience. Bundled words and background audio are loaded locally. This version has no account system, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not upload these local records to a developer-operated server.

The Google Play version does not require a general privacy-consent gate and does not save a consent record simply because you open the app. Existing local records from an earlier installation may remain until you clear the app data.

Clipboard and external applications

If a mail application cannot be opened, you can choose Copy email to place our support address on the system clipboard. The app does not read the clipboard or copy text in the background. The operating system and other apps may handle clipboard contents under their own rules.

Contact us opens your mail application with our address and a localized feedback subject. You decide whether to send a message. If you send one, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you provide, and use them to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Your mail provider processes the message under its own policy.

Rate app opens the device's app market for this application's identifier. External mail and store applications are governed by their own privacy policies. Flutter and the included file-storage, audio-playback and link-launching libraries provide app functionality; this version does not integrate an in-app payment service or cloud speech service.

Retention, security and your choices

Local records remain on your device until you remove or reset the relevant information or clear the app's storage. You can restore blocked words and change settings in the app. You can delete all local app records using Android's Clear storage function. Uninstalling normally removes private app storage; operating-system backup or restore settings may preserve or restore data, and are controlled by Android and your device provider. We use the app's private storage but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence we control. There is no app account to delete. We do not sell local app records or use them for advertising.

Children and policy updates

The app is intended for adults. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate. We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the date and the in-app text. Material changes will be communicated where required.

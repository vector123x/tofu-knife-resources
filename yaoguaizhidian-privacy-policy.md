# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Thank you for using **妖怪知典**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

This app is a Chinese-language offline reference app about Chinese mythical creatures, ancient monsters, spirits, and related folklore.

## 1. Data Collection

This app does not require account registration or login.

We do not collect, upload, store, sell, or share your personal information.

The app does not collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, audio, payment card details, or other personally identifiable information.

## 2. Local Data

Some app features may save data locally on your device, such as:

* Favorite entries
* Browsing history
* Search history
* App settings

This data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

You can delete this local data by using the app’s built-in options, clearing app data in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

## 3. Permissions

This app does not request sensitive permissions such as contacts, SMS, phone, camera, microphone, or precise location.

## 4. Payments

This app is distributed as a paid app through Google Play.

All purchases, refunds, taxes, and payment processing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card number, payment password, or other sensitive payment details.

## 5. Third-Party Services

This app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, third-party login services, or cloud sync services.

Please note that Google Play may process certain information related to app downloads, purchases, refunds, crash reports, or device compatibility according to Google’s own policies.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

This app is not specifically directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the app’s features or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
